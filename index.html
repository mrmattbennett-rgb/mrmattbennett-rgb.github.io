Complete Financial Transparency

How Mediterranean Yacht Charter Pricing Works

Under MYBA (Mediterranean Yachting Brokers Association) terms, the base weekly charter rate covers the exclusive hire of the yacht, full crew wages, insurance, and all onboard water toys. Fuel, bespoke food & fine wine provisioning, and marina dockage are managed transparently through your APA (Advance Provisioning Allowance — typically 30%), with all unused funds refunded to you at disembarkation.
